Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your JPG files are never uploaded to any server and are automatically deleted when you close the page.

Why convert JPG to PDF?

PDF is universally compatible, supports multiple pages, and is the standard format for sharing documents. Converting JPG to PDF makes your images look professional and easy to share.

Can I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?

Yes! When you select multiple JPG files, each image will be converted to a separate PDF. If you want to combine them into one PDF, use the "Merge PDF" tool after conversion.

Will I lose image quality?

Using the "High" quality setting preserves maximum image quality. The PDF will maintain the original image resolution and color accuracy.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes! Drag and drop multiple JPG files or select multiple files using the file picker. Each image will be converted individually, and you can download them all at once as a ZIP file.
