Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

What PDF types work best?

Text-based PDFs work best. Scanned/image-based PDFs require OCR and may produce limited results.

Are images preserved?

Currently, this tool extracts text only. Images are not included in the Markdown output.

Will headings and formatting be preserved?

Yes! The converter tries to detect headings (by font size), lists (bullets and numbers), and paragraphs. You can adjust detection sensitivity in the options.

What about tables?

Simple tables may be converted to Markdown tables. Complex tables may lose their structure.
