Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Droplet, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our file conversion tools.

1. Your Files Are Private

Your documents are never uploaded to our servers. All file processing happens entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript libraries. This means:

  • Your files never leave your device
  • Your files are never transmitted over the internet
  • Your files are never stored on our servers
  • Your files are automatically deleted when you close your browser tab
  • We have no access to your files at any time

2. What Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal data. However, like most websites, we use third-party services that may collect anonymous usage information:

  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymous usage data to help us understand how visitors interact with our website. This includes page views, time on site, and referral sources. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Google AdSense: Displays relevant ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Cloudflare: Our hosting provider may collect anonymous usage data for security and performance optimization.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and are independent of Droplet.

3. Cookies

We use cookies for essential functionality and analytics:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our website (Google Analytics)
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to serve relevant ads

You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings. For more information, visit AboutCookies.org.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to improve your experience:

  • Google AdSense – for advertising
  • Google Analytics – for anonymous usage analytics
  • Cloudflare – for hosting, security, and performance

These services are governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies to understand how they handle your data.

5. Data Security

Since we don't collect or store your files, the primary security concern is protecting your connection to our website. We implement:

  • HTTPS encryption for secure page delivery
  • Client-side processing in your browser's sandbox environment
  • Industry-standard security practices for our infrastructure

6. Children's Privacy

Droplet is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated date.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any data we hold about you (which is none, as we don't collect personal data)
  • Request deletion of any data we hold (none exists)
  • Opt out of cookies using your browser settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google Ad Settings
  • Use ad-blocking software to limit ad tracking

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@dropletweb.xyz
  • Website: Contact Page
