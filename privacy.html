Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Droplet, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit Droplet and use our document, image, and file-processing tools.

1. Your Files Are Private

Droplet is designed to process supported files locally in your browser using client-side technologies.

For tools that operate entirely in your browser:

  • Your files are not uploaded to Droplet's servers.
  • Droplet does not store copies of your files on its servers.
  • Droplet does not have access to the contents of your files.
  • Processing occurs locally on your device.
  • Temporary file data used by the tool is generally cleared when you close or refresh the page, depending on your browser.

Where a particular feature requires external processing in the future, we will clearly disclose this before the feature is used and update this Privacy Policy where appropriate.

2. Information About Website Usage

While Droplet does not collect or store the contents of files processed locally in your browser, our website and third-party service providers may process certain technical and usage information.

Depending on the services enabled on Droplet, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location derived from an IP address
  • Referral source
  • Date and time of visits
  • Cookie or similar technology identifiers
  • Information about how visitors interact with the website

We may use this information to operate, secure, understand, and improve Droplet.

3. Analytics

Droplet may use Google Analytics or similar analytics services to understand how visitors use the website.

Analytics providers may collect information such as page views, approximate location, device and browser information, referral sources, and interactions with the website.

This information helps us understand website performance and improve Droplet's tools and user experience.

4. Advertising

Droplet may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to display advertisements.

If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Deliver and measure advertisements
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Personalize advertisements where permitted and where the appropriate consent has been obtained

The information processed by advertising providers is governed by their own privacy policies and settings.

Users can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services through Google's privacy resources and advertising settings.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Droplet and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Essential functionality — to provide or secure website features.
  • Analytics — to understand website usage and performance.
  • Advertising — if advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled.

Where required by applicable law, Droplet will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Droplet may use third-party services to operate, secure, analyze, and support the website. These may include:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, content delivery, security, and performance.
  • Google Analytics — website analytics, if enabled.
  • Google AdSense — advertising, if enabled.

These providers may process certain information according to their own privacy policies. Droplet does not control how independent third-party providers process information under their own policies.

7. Data Security

Droplet uses reasonable technical measures designed to protect the website and its users, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for communication with the website
  • Client-side processing for supported file tools
  • Browser security mechanisms
  • Security and performance services provided by our infrastructure providers

No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Droplet is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children through our file-processing tools. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding personal information processed about you.

These may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other controls provided under applicable privacy laws.

You may also:

  • Manage cookies through your browser settings.
  • Adjust Google's advertising preferences.
  • Decline non-essential cookies where a consent mechanism is provided.
  • Use browser privacy features or content-blocking tools.

Because supported Droplet tools process files locally, Droplet does not have copies of those files to access, retrieve, or delete from its servers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Droplet, our service providers, or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date shown at the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy at Droplet, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@dropletweb.xyz
  • Website: Contact Page
