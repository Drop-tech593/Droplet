Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Droplet, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our file conversion tools.

1. Information We Collect

We DO NOT collect or store any personal data. All file processing happens entirely in your browser. We do not:

  • Upload your files to any server
  • Store or cache your files
  • Collect your email address or personal information
  • Track your browsing activity across other websites

2. How We Process Your Files

All file conversions are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript libraries. Your files never leave your device. This means:

  • Your files are never transmitted over the internet
  • Your files are never stored on our servers
  • Your files are never accessed by any third party
  • Your files are automatically deleted when you close your browser tab

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to improve your experience:

  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display relevant ads. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Cloudflare: We use Cloudflare to host our website and improve performance. Cloudflare may collect anonymous usage data for security and analytics.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This data is anonymized and does not identify you personally.

4. Cookies

We use cookies for essential functionality and analytics:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our website
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to serve relevant ads

You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings. For more information, visit AboutCookies.org.

5. Data Security

Since we don't collect or store any of your files, there is no data to secure. All processing happens locally on your device using:

  • HTTPS encryption for secure page delivery
  • Client-side processing in your browser's sandbox environment
  • Industry-standard security practices for our infrastructure

6. Children's Privacy

Droplet is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated date.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any data we hold about you (which is none)
  • Request deletion of any data we hold (none exists)
  • Opt out of cookies using your browser settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google Ad Settings

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@droplet.com
  • Website: Contact Page
