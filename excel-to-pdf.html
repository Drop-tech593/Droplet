What is Excel to PDF conversion?

Excel is the standard spreadsheet format for numbers, tables, formulas, and charts. Because Excel is editable, spreadsheets can be changed accidentally or look different depending on the software used. PDF is a fixed-layout format where every cell has an exact position on the page.

Converting Excel to PDF locks the spreadsheet into a consistent visual output. Reports look identical on every device, recipients cannot change the data, and the file can be printed reliably. It is the standard way to share financial statements, invoices, project schedules, budgets, and data handouts.

When should you use this tool?

  • You have a financial report or budget in Excel and need a shareable, non-editable version.
  • You want to send a spreadsheet to someone who does not have Excel installed.
  • You need to print a spreadsheet with consistent page breaks and margins.
  • You want to archive a snapshot of your data before it changes.
  • You want the conversion to happen entirely on your device, without uploading your spreadsheet anywhere.

How the converter works

The Excel file is opened in the browser using SheetJS, which reads the raw XLSX or XLS data. For each sheet, the full used range is extracted — including cells that are outside the visible viewport in Excel. Merged cells are read from the file's !merges metadata. Column widths and row heights are extracted from !cols and !rows and applied to an HTML table.

The rendered table is rasterised to a canvas and sliced into A4, Letter, or Legal pages. Each page is embedded as a JPEG image in a PDF built with jsPDF. The whole process runs client-side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my Excel data safe?

Yes. All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your Excel file is never uploaded to any server and is removed from memory when you close the page.

Is this Excel to PDF converter free?

Yes, the converter is completely free and does not require a sign-up or account.

What Excel formats are supported?

Both .xlsx (Excel 2007 and later) and .xls (Excel 97-2003) formats are supported.

Will my formatting be preserved?

Cell values, the full used range of each sheet, merged cells, column widths, and row heights are preserved. Visual styling such as fills, fonts, and borders is not reproduced in this version.

Can I convert multiple sheets at once?

Yes. You can choose to include all sheets or only the active sheet in the PDF output.

What page sizes are supported?

A4, Letter, and Legal are supported, in both Portrait and Landscape orientation.

Can I copy text out of the PDF?

Not in this version. Pages are rendered as images, so text cannot be selected or searched. This is a known limitation that will be addressed in a future update.

What is the maximum file size?

The tool accepts Excel files up to 50 MB. Because everything runs locally, the practical limit is your device's available memory.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, all parsing and conversion run locally in your browser. You can convert Excel files without an internet connection.

Will my formulas be preserved?

Formulas are recalculated to their values by SheetJS. The PDF contains the computed cell values, not the formulas themselves.
