AI Summarizer

Turn long articles, documents, and text into concise, easy-to-read summaries — right in your browser.

⚠️

Read this before using

  • It's extractive, not generative. This tool selects the most important existing sentences from your text. It does not write new sentences or rephrase ideas like ChatGPT.
  • No internet / no API. Everything runs locally in your browser. There is no server, no account, and no external AI call.
  • Works best on structured prose. Articles, essays, reports, and documentation summarize well. Poetry, code, chat logs, and tables do not.
  • English-optimized. Stop-word lists and sentence splitting work best on English. Other languages may produce lower-quality results.
  • Not a substitute for reading. Always verify important details in the original text. Summaries can miss nuance, sarcasm, and context.
  • Your text never leaves your device. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored. Refreshing the page clears everything.
