Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

Will my formatting be preserved?

The converter rebuilds editable text, preserves page boundaries, detects formatting, and inserts PDF images into the Word document.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scanned/image-based PDFs require OCR. This tool works best with text-based PDFs.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB without issues.
