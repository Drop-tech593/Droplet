Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

Will my formatting be preserved?

The converter uses professional Word formatting: 14pt bold headings, 11pt justified body text, single line spacing, and proper margins.

What about images and tables?

Images are extracted and embedded in the DOCX file. Tables are detected and converted to Word tables with proper borders and formatting.

Can I convert large PDFs?

Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB. Very large documents with many images may take longer to process.
