Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

Will my formatting be preserved?

The conversion engine preserves text, fonts, tables, and basic layout. However, complex PDF layouts (multi-column, advanced graphics) may have some formatting differences. For best results, use simple, text-heavy PDFs.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scanned/image-based PDFs require OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract text. This tool works best with text-based PDFs. For scanned documents, consider using a dedicated OCR tool first.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB without issues. Very large files may take longer to process.

How does the conversion work?

Droplet uses a WebAssembly-based library that runs a professional PDF-to-Word conversion engine directly in your browser. This ensures your data stays private while delivering high-quality results.
