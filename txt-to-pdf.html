Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your text file is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

Can I customize the PDF?

Yes! You can choose page size (A4, Letter, Legal), font size (10-14pt), line spacing (1.5-2.0), and whether to include a title page.

What about special characters?

The converter supports UTF-8 encoding, so special characters, accents, and non-Latin scripts should display correctly.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle text files up to 10MB without issues.

How does the conversion work?

The text is read, formatted with proper fonts and margins, and rendered as HTML. Then html2pdf.js converts it to a downloadable PDF file. All processing stays in your browser.
