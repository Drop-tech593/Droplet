Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

How does the conversion work?

Each page of your PDF is rendered as a high-quality image and placed on a PowerPoint slide. This preserves the exact layout and formatting of your original document.

Will the text be editable?

The conversion preserves the visual appearance as images on each slide. For fully editable text, consider using the PDF to DOCX converter instead.

Can I choose the slide layout?

Yes! You can choose between Standard (4:3) and Widescreen (16:9) layouts, as well as adjust image quality and background color.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 50MB without issues.
