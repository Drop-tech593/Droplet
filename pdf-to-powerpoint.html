Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

How are slides generated?

Each PDF page is rendered as a high-resolution image and placed on a dedicated PowerPoint slide, preserving the original layout perfectly.

Can I edit the text in PowerPoint?

The slides contain page images, so text isn't directly editable. You can add text boxes, shapes, or annotations on top of each slide in PowerPoint.

Are there file size limits?

Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB. Very large documents with many pages may take longer to process.
