Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

What kind of PDFs work best?

Text-based BOQ PDFs with a clear header row containing Item No, Unit, Quantity, Rate and Amount. Scanned or image-only PDFs are not supported.

Does it preserve the document structure?

Yes. Section headers, bill titles and "Carried Forward" footers are preserved as their own rows, styled appropriately.

What about pages without a BOQ table?

Cover pages, instruction pages, and summary tables are automatically skipped. Only pages containing a recognisable BOQ header are extracted.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 50MB.
