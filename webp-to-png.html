WebP vs PNG Comparison

🟢 WebP

  • ✅ Smaller file size (up to 30% less than PNG)
  • ✅ Supports transparency
  • ✅ Supports animation
  • ❌ Limited browser/device support
  • ❌ Not compatible with older software

🔵 PNG

  • ✅ Universal compatibility
  • ✅ Lossless quality
  • ✅ Supports transparency
  • ✅ Supported everywhere
  • ❌ Larger file size than WebP

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your WebP files are never uploaded to any server and are automatically deleted when you close the page.

Why convert WebP to PNG?

PNG is universally compatible and supports lossless quality and transparency. Converting WebP to PNG ensures your images work everywhere, including older browsers, design software, and print applications.

Will I lose image quality?

No! PNG is a lossless format, so no quality is lost during conversion. The converted image will be an exact pixel-perfect representation of your original WebP image.

What about transparency?

PNG supports transparency (alpha channel). If your WebP image has transparent areas, they will be preserved. You can also choose to add a white or black background if needed.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes! Drag and drop multiple WebP files or select multiple files using the file picker. Each image will be converted individually, and you can download them all at once as a ZIP file.
