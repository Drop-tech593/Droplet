HEIC is Apple's modern image format used on iPhones and iPads. It offers better compression than JPG but isn't compatible with all devices and platforms. Converting to JPG ensures universal compatibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your HEIC files are never uploaded to any server and are automatically deleted when you close the page.

Why convert HEIC to JPG?

HEIC files have better compression but are not universally supported. Converting to JPG ensures your images can be viewed on any device, shared easily, and uploaded to any website or platform.

Will I lose image quality?

JPG is a lossy format, so some quality is lost during conversion. However, using the "Maximum" quality setting preserves as much detail as possible. The "Very High" setting offers a good balance of quality and file size.

What output formats are supported?

You can convert HEIC to JPG (most compatible), PNG (lossless, larger files), or WebP (modern, better compression).

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes! Drag and drop multiple HEIC files or select multiple files using the file picker. Each image will be converted individually, and you can download them all at once as a ZIP file.
