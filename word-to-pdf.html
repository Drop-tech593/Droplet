Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All processing happens locally in your browser. Your Word document is never uploaded to any server.

Will my formatting be preserved?

The converter preserves fonts, tables, images, and page layout. Complex Word features may have limitations.

What file types are supported?

Currently supports .docx files. Other Word formats may work but are not guaranteed.

What about images in the document?

Images are preserved and included in the PDF output. Large images may affect file size.
