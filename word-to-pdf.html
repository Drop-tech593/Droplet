Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your Word document is never uploaded to any server.

Will my formatting be preserved?

The conversion engine preserves fonts, tables, images, headers, footers, hyperlinks, and page layout.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle Word files up to 50MB without issues.
