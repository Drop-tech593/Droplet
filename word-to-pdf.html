Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your Word document is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

Will my formatting be preserved?

The conversion engine preserves fonts, tables, images, headers, footers, hyperlinks, and page layout. Complex Word features like SmartArt or advanced drawing objects may have limitations [citation:4][citation:8]. For best results, use standard text and tables.

What about scanned or image-based documents?

This tool works best with text-based Word documents. For scanned documents, you may need an OCR tool first to extract the text.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle Word files up to 50MB without issues.
