Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All text extraction happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scanned/image-based PDFs require OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract text. This tool works best with text-based PDFs. For scanned documents, consider using a dedicated OCR tool first.

Will formatting be preserved?

This tool extracts plain text only. Formatting like bold, italics, and fonts are not preserved. If you need formatted text, use the PDF to DOCX converter instead.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB without issues. Very large files may take longer to process.

What can I use the extracted text for?

Extracted text can be used for: copying content into documents, analyzing data, searching through text, feeding into AI tools, or converting to other formats.
