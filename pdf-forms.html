Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use PDF Forms?

Upload a PDF, then click any field type in the toolbar to add a form field. Drag fields to reposition, click to configure them, and export when done.

Can I fill existing PDF forms?

Yes! If your PDF has interactive form fields, they will be detected and you can fill them. You can also add new fields on top of any PDF.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDF and form data never leave your device.

What field types are supported?

Text inputs, checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns, date pickers, and signature fields.
