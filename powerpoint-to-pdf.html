Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your PowerPoint files are never uploaded to any server.

Will animations be preserved?

PDF is a static format. Animations, transitions, and interactive elements are not preserved. Each slide becomes a static page in the PDF.

What about fonts and images?

Text, images, and basic formatting are preserved. For best results, use standard fonts. Custom fonts may be substituted if not embedded.

What file formats are supported?

Currently supports .PPTX (PowerPoint 2007 and later). Legacy .PPT files may work but are not guaranteed.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle presentations up to 100MB without issues.
