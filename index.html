Questions & answers

Privacy, file limits and offline use, explained.

How do I convert a file?

Select a file on the homepage, choose a matching tool, and follow the conversion options on that tool’s page. You can also open a tool first and select a file there.

Are my files uploaded?

Most conversion tools process files in your browser. PDF translation sends extracted text to MyMemory only after you enable online translation. Voice transcription offers an on-device model; the optional browser speech service may send audio to its provider.

What are the file-size limits?

Limits vary by tool and your device’s memory. JSON to CSV accepts up to 10 MB, for example. Matching tools show their limits before a homepage transfer, which is limited to 100 MB in total.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG without losing quality?

Droplet can create high-quality JPG images from HEIC photos. JPG uses lossy compression, so a conversion cannot guarantee identical image data. Choose a higher quality setting when detail matters.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account or subscription is required to use the tools. Some online features depend on external services and their availability or usage limits.

Can I use Droplet offline?

Many tools work offline after their page and required resources have loaded. Speech models, OCR language data and online translation may need an internet connection. Test the specific tool before relying on it offline.

Does Droplet store my files?

A homepage transfer briefly stores a copy on your device, deletes it after the destination tool receives it, and expires unused transfers after two minutes. Expired copies are removed while Droplet is open or on your next visit. Voice drafts, preferences and downloaded models can remain in browser storage until you clear them.

How do I report a problem?

Open Contact, include the tool name and what happened, and avoid sending the document itself unless the site owner specifically needs it. The contact page links to the existing privacy email and Droplet’s public profiles.
