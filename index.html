Drop your HEIC file

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about converting files with Droplet.

How do I convert HEIC to JPG without losing quality?

▾

Use Droplet's HEIC to JPG converter. Drop your .heic file, choose JPG output, and download. Our converter uses lossless decoding with a 92% JPEG quality setting — visually identical to the original, but 100% compatible with every device, browser, and app.

Is HEIC to JPG conversion free without signing in?

▾

Yes — Droplet is 100% free, requires no account, no email, and no sign-in. There are no hidden limits, no watermarks, and no upsells. Every conversion runs entirely inside your browser.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

▾

No. Droplet is fully client-side. Files are processed using JavaScript and WebAssembly inside your browser, so nothing is ever uploaded, stored, or shared. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and conversions will still work.

Is there a file size limit for conversions?

▾

There is no server-side limit because files never leave your device. Practical limits depend on your device's RAM — most modern browsers handle files up to 2GB without issues.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone or Android?

▾

Yes. Droplet works in any modern mobile browser — Safari (iOS), Chrome (Android), Firefox, and Edge. You can also install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for an app-like experience with offline support.

Do I need to install any software?

▾

No installation required. Droplet runs entirely in your web browser. Optionally, you can install it as a PWA for offline use — look for the "Install" button in the header or your browser's address bar.

What other formats does Droplet support?

▾

Over 100+ formats including PDF, DOCX, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, PNG, JPG, SVG, PSD, RAW, TIFF, EPUB, TXT, JSON, CSV, XML, and more. Browse all converters.

Can I use Droplet offline?

▾

Yes — if you install Droplet as a PWA, it caches its assets and can work completely offline. Your files never need an internet connection anyway, since everything runs locally.

Does Droplet add watermarks to my files?

▾

Never. Your converted files are clean — no watermarks, no branding, no metadata injection. What you convert is what you get.

How is Droplet different from other converters?

▾

Most online converters upload your files to a server, process them, then delete them — but you have to trust them. Droplet never uploads anything. Everything runs in your browser, so there's nothing to trust: your files literally cannot leave your device.
