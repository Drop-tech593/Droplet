Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All splitting happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

What split modes are available?

You can split by "Every N Pages" (e.g., every 2 pages), "Custom Ranges" (e.g., 1-3, 5, 7-9), or "Extract Selected Pages" by clicking on pages.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB without issues.
