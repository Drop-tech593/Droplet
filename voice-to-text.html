Voice to Text — Free, Private, Browser-Based Transcription

Convert speech to text instantly using your device's microphone or by uploading an audio file. This tool runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server, no sign-up required, and no file size limits beyond your device's memory.

Whether you're dictating notes, transcribing an interview, or creating subtitles for a video, the workflow is simple: choose a language, pick a model, hit record or upload, and watch the transcript appear in real time.

Live Microphone Transcription

Click Start Recording and begin speaking. The tool captures audio in small chunks, transcribes each chunk using the Whisper model, and appends the results to the transcript area. The timer shows how long you've been recording, and the progress bar fills as each chunk is processed.

You can pause at any time — the transcript is preserved. When you're done, click Stop and the final text is ready to copy or download.

Upload Audio Files

Drag and drop any audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, WEBM) onto the upload zone, or click to browse. The tool decodes the audio, splits it into manageable chunks, and transcribes each one. Progress is shown in real time, with elapsed time and estimated remaining time.

This is ideal for transcribing interviews, lectures, meeting recordings, or voice memos — without uploading sensitive audio to a third-party server.

Multi-Language Support

Choose from a wide range of languages including English (US/UK), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, and Hindi. The model adapts to the selected language for better accuracy.

Export Options

Once your transcript is ready, you can:

  • Copy to clipboard — paste into any text editor or document.
  • Download as TXT — plain text file for universal compatibility.
  • Download as DOCX — formatted Word document (coming soon).
  • Download as SRT — subtitle file with timestamps for video projects.
  • Download as JSON — structured data with segments and timestamps.

Privacy — Your Audio Never Leaves Your Device

All audio processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly and the Web Audio API. The Whisper model runs entirely on your device. No audio data, transcript text, or metadata is ever transmitted to any server. The only external resource loaded is the open-source model file, which is cached by your browser after the first load.

You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool will continue to work.

What This Tool Does Not Do

  • It does not store your transcripts. Everything is ephemeral — close the tab and it's gone.
  • It does not support real-time translation. It transcribes in the selected language, not translates.
  • It does not provide speaker diarization in this version. All text is attributed to a single speaker.
  • It does not work offline on the first load. The model file must be downloaded once, then it's cached.

Voice to Text Questions

Is this voice to text tool really free?

Yes. The tool is completely free to use with no hidden limits. There are no sign-ups, no watermarks, and no file count restrictions. The only practical limit is your device's memory and processing power.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes. The interface is fully responsive and works on modern iOS and Android browsers. Microphone access requires a secure context (HTTPS or localhost) and user permission.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on the model, audio quality, and background noise. The Whisper Base model provides good accuracy for clear speech in quiet environments. For best results, use a external microphone and speak clearly at a moderate pace.

Can I transcribe a long recording?

Yes. The tool splits audio into chunks and processes them sequentially. A 2-hour recording will work, but it may take several minutes to process depending on your device's speed.

What audio formats are supported?

The tool accepts any format your browser can decode: MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, WEBM, and more. If your browser can play it, the tool can transcribe it.

Is my audio uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your audio never leaves your device. The only external request is to download the Whisper model file, which is cached after the first load.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes. The transcripts you generate are yours to use however you wish. The tool itself is provided as-is for personal and commercial use.

Why is the first transcription slow?

On the first use, the Whisper model must be downloaded (about 150 MB for the Base model). After that, it's cached by your browser and subsequent transcriptions start immediately.

Does it support punctuation and capitalization?

The Whisper model includes basic punctuation and capitalization. For best results, speak in complete sentences with natural pauses.

Can I edit the transcript after it's generated?

Yes. The transcript area is a standard text area — you can click anywhere and edit the text directly. Changes are not saved automatically, so copy or download before closing.

What happens if I close the tab during transcription?

The transcription stops and any unsaved text is lost. Always copy or download your transcript before closing the tab.

Is there a word or character limit?

No hard limit. The practical limit is your device's available memory. Very long transcriptions (100,000+ words) may become sluggish to edit in the browser.
