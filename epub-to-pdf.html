What is EPUB to PDF conversion?

EPUB is the standard reflowable eBook format. It is essentially a ZIP archive containing XHTML content, CSS styles, images, and metadata. Because it reflows, the same EPUB looks different on every screen — a phone might show short paragraphs with wide margins, while a tablet shows dense text.

PDF is the opposite: a fixed-layout format where every element has an exact position on the page. Converting EPUB to PDF locks the book into a consistent layout that looks identical on every device, which is useful for printing, sharing, archiving, or reading on a device that does not support EPUB.

When should you use this tool?

  • You have a DRM-free EPUB and want to read it on a device that only supports PDF.
  • You want to print an eBook or share it as a fixed-layout document.
  • You want to archive the content of an EPUB so it renders the same way forever.
  • You want to share a chapter or a whole book with someone who does not have an e-reader.
  • You want the conversion to happen entirely on your own device, without uploading your book to any server.

How the converter works

The EPUB is unzipped in the browser. The container.xml file is read to find the OPF package document, which lists every chapter, image, and stylesheet. Metadata — title, author, publisher — is extracted from the OPF. Each chapter in the spine is read in order, its body content is extracted, images are inlined as base64 data URLs, and the result is a self-contained HTML fragment per chapter.

For the PDF, each chapter is laid out on A4 pages. Blocks that fit on one page are placed directly. Blocks that overflow are split at the nearest paragraph or heading boundary, so text flows naturally from one page to the next without cutting lines in half.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your EPUB file is never uploaded to any server and is removed from memory when you close the page.

Is this EPUB to PDF converter free?

Yes, the converter is completely free and does not require a sign-up or account.

Will my formatting be preserved?

Fonts, headings, paragraphs, tables, and images are preserved. Chapters are paginated into A4 pages with proper margins and page breaks.

Can I convert DRM-protected EPUBs?

No. The tool only works with DRM-free EPUB files. DRM-protected eBooks must have their DRM removed by a legal tool before conversion.

What is the maximum EPUB file size?

The tool accepts EPUB files up to 50 MB. Because everything runs locally, the practical limit is your device's available memory.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, all parsing and conversion run locally in your browser. You can convert EPUBs without an internet connection.

Will the chapter structure be preserved?

Yes. Each chapter or spine item becomes its own section in the PDF, with a title page at the start and clean page breaks between chapters.

What about fonts and images?

Images are extracted from the EPUB and embedded directly into the PDF as base64 data. Fonts fall back to Times New Roman or another serif font for readability, unless the EPUB specifies embedded fonts.

Can I convert multiple EPUBs at once?

The current version handles one EPUB at a time. You can return to the tool and convert another EPUB after each download.

What is the difference between EPUB and PDF?

EPUB is a reflowable eBook format designed for e-readers, with adjustable font size and layout. PDF is a fixed-layout format designed for printing and consistent display across devices.
