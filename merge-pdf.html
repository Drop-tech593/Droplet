Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All merging happens locally in your browser. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server.

Will page sizes be preserved?

Yes. Each page keeps its original dimensions (A4, Letter, A3, landscape, etc.).

Can I reorder pages inside a PDF?

Yes! Tap "Manage Pages" on any card to reorder, rotate, duplicate, or delete individual pages.

Can I add pages from another PDF?

Yes! Inside Manage Pages, use the "Add Pages" button to insert pages from any other PDF you've uploaded.
