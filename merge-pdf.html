Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All merging happens locally in your browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded to any server.

Can I reorder the files?

Yes! Simply drag and drop the files in the list to reorder them before merging.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB each without issues.
