Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All merging happens locally in your browser. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server.

Will page sizes be preserved?

Yes. Each page keeps its original dimensions (A4, Letter, A3, landscape, etc.). Nothing is forced onto a standard size.

Can I reorder pages inside a PDF?

Yes! Click "Manage Pages" on any card to reorder, rotate, duplicate, or delete individual pages.

What about password-protected PDFs?

This tool cannot merge password-protected PDFs. You'll need to remove the password first.
