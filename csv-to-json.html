Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your CSV data is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

What CSV formats are supported?

This converter supports comma-separated (CSV), tab-separated (TSV), and custom delimiters. It automatically detects headers and handles quoted fields.

Can I convert Excel files?

This tool works with CSV files. For Excel files (.xlsx), you'll need to export them as CSV first from Excel or use a dedicated Excel to JSON converter.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle CSV files up to 10MB without issues.

What if my CSV has no headers?

The converter automatically detects headers from the first row. If your CSV has no headers, the data will be converted with generic keys like "col0", "col1", etc.
