Frequently Asked Questions

Is my file safe?

Yes! Everything happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server.

Can I choose where numbers appear?

Yes. You can place numbers at any of 6 positions: Top Left, Top Center, Top Right, Bottom Left, Bottom Center, or Bottom Right.

Will the quality of my PDF change?

No. The original text, images, and vector graphics stay exactly the same — we only add text on top.

Can I skip the first page?

Yes. Enable "Skip first page" to leave the cover page without a number — perfect for books and reports.
