Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes! All conversions happen locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to any server and is automatically deleted when you close the page.

Why convert PDF to JPG?

Converting PDF to JPG allows you to extract individual pages as images. This is useful for sharing specific pages, creating thumbnails, or using PDF content in presentations and documents.

Will I lose image quality?

JPG is a lossy format, so some quality is lost during conversion. Using the "Maximum" quality setting preserves as much detail as possible. For highest quality, consider using the PDF to PNG converter instead.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs work perfectly! Each page is rendered as an image, preserving the exact appearance of the scanned document.

Are there file size limits?

Since everything runs locally, limits depend on your device's memory. Most modern browsers handle PDFs up to 100MB without issues. Very large files may take longer to process.
